PROJECT BASED LEARNING: SYNOPSIS
DEBTTRACKER (DEBTSYNC)


Smart inter-student ledger, interest engine, and debt management portal for students
Manual, informal tracking of money lent and borrowed among university students frequently leads to accounting discrepancies, forgotten debts, and social friction.
"DebtTracker" is a full-stack, web-based inter-student financial management system designed to bring transparency and automation to student micro-loans and shared ledgers.
The platform converts informal cash transfers into a secure, centralized digital ledger where both creditors (lenders) and debtors (borrowers) maintain real-time visibility over active balances.
Its primary objective is two-fold: to empower lenders to effortlessly record interest-free or interest-bearing credit (supporting simple and continuous compound interest models across weekly, monthly, or yearly schedules) and to provide debtors with an open self-service lookup portal where they can check their live dues, review repayment histories, process smart bulk payments, and inspect dynamic financial growth charts.


Existing System and Need for System
Currently, university students ( or anybody else)  rely on fragmented, unstructured methods to track expenses and credit, such as physical notebooks, mental notes, Excel spreadsheets, or casual chat logs in messaging apps.


Drawbacks of Existing Systems:
* Lack of Real-Time Visibility: Debtors rarely know their exact outstanding balance without asking the lender directly, leading to miscommunication.
* Manual Calculation Errors:Calculating simple or compound interest by hand over different periods is tedious and prone to mistakes.
* Settlement Bottlenecks: Clearing multiple distinct debt entries simultaneously involves manual computations to figure out which credit repayments should take priority.
* No Verification Trail: Informal agreements lack clear repayment logs or audit activity histories, making disputes hard to resolve.
The Need: The core objective of the platform is to simplify peer-to-peer financial obligations by providing an organized framework for tracking expenditures, managing credit agreements, and calculating interest. Furthermore, there is a critical need for an open-source, trustable platform that ensures security and transparency. Consequently, a centralized, readily accessible web portal is essential to handle automated interest computations, efficient batch settlement allocations, and transparent interactive dashboards for monitoring all financial liabilities comprehensively from initiation to closure.
Scope of System
* Role-Based Access & Identity Lookup: Phone number and name-based lookup mechanism with built-in disambiguation to view personal ledger details without complex onboarding barriers.
* Flexible Debt Engine: Creation and management of debt records supporting interest-free models along with custom interest-bearing records (simple and exponential compound interest).
* Debtor Self-Service Portal: An independent, full-screen dashboard for borrowers to view total outstanding dues, principal totals, and total repaid funds.
* Smart Bulk Payment Allocation: An automated payment engine that distributes bulk batch payments across multiple active debts, prioritizing highest-interest records first to minimize total borrower liability.
* Visual Analytics & Live Accrual Speed Ticker: Real-time metrics showing exact daily and monthly interest growth rates (+₹/day, +₹/month), financial split visual bars (Principal vs. Interest vs. Paid), and interactive time-series growth projection graphs (30D, 90D, 1Y).
* Audit Activity & Repayment Logs: Immutable activity tracking capturing debt creation, partial payments, note updates, and full settlements.

Proposed System
The proposed "DebtTracker" system entirely replaces manual, error-prone record-keeping with an automated web platform.
By combining a responsive vanilla JavaScript frontend with a cloud-hosted relational PostgreSQL database, the system automates the entire micro-loan lifecycle: from initial debt logging and dynamic compound interest accrual to borrower self-service lookups and algorithmic bulk payment settlement.


Feasibility Study
   * Technical Feasibility: The project utilizes standard, proven web standards (HTML/CSS/JS, PostgreSQL, Supabase SDK, Chart.js). The serverless cloud infrastructure eliminates manual server maintenance, making technical execution highly feasible.
   * Economic Feasibility: The system relies entirely on open-source libraries and cloud free-tier hosting services (Supabase PostgreSQL), incurring zero operational or software licensing costs during development.
   * Operational Feasibility: The clean, mobile-first interface and zero-friction phone/name lookup system ensure students can adopt and interact with the platform seamlessly without formal training.


Objectives of Proposed System
   * To eliminate manual bookkeeping errors and interpersonal disputes through automated, mathematically precise interest accrual.
   * To provide borrowers with a transparent, self-service portal to check live balances, accrual speeds, and payment histories anytime.
   * To streamline debt clearance using smart bulk payment allocation algorithms that pay off high-interest debts first.
   * To empower users with intuitive visual analytics, including daily/monthly accrual tickers and multi-range financial growth projection charts.
Users of System
   * Lenders (Creditors): Students creating debt entries, setting interest terms (0% or simple/compound rates), evaluating overall portfolio yields, and verifying incoming payments.
   * Borrowers (Debtors): Students looking up their account via phone/name to inspect current dues, view interest breakdowns, track repayments, and submit bulk settlements.
   * Administrators: Platform owners managing user entries, PIN-protected admin portfolio dashboards, database integrity, and system-wide activity logs.
